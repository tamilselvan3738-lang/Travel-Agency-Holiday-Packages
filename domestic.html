Featured Signature Itinerary

Royal Golden Triangle & Palace Odyssey

6 Days • 5 Nights

Destination Highlights

Private dawn viewing of the Taj Mahal without crowds, elephant sanctuary interaction at Dera Amer, luxury royal lake cruise in Udaipur, and imperial dining in Jaipur’s City Palace.

Day 01
Arrival in Delhi • Imperial Heritage Tour

Chauffeur reception at airport. Check-in to The Imperial Delhi. Afternoon private tour of Humayun’s Tomb and India Gate, followed by a welcome dinner.

Day 02
Express to Agra • Sunset View of Taj Mahal

High-speed train journey to Agra. Check-in to ITC Mughal. Afternoon exploration of Agra Fort and private sunset tea at Mehtab Bagh overlooking the marble monument.

Day 03
VIP Dawn at Taj Mahal • Drive to Jaipur

Exclusive dawn entrance to Taj Mahal. Post breakfast, scenic private Mercedes transfer to Jaipur via the ghost city of Fatehpur Sikri. Check-in to Rambagh Palace.

Day 04
The Pink City • Amber Fort & Royal Observatories

Jeep ascent to Amber Fort with private royal historian. Photography stop at Hawa Mahal, private courtyards of City Palace, and bespoke gems & textile artisans.

Day 05
Flight to Udaipur • Lake Pichola Royal Cruise

Morning flight to the City of Lakes. Check-in to Taj Lake Palace. Sunset private royal boat charter across Lake Pichola with candlelit dinner on the water.

Day 06
Saheliyon-ki-Bari • Departure Transfer

Leisurely morning breakfast by the lake, visit to the royal gardens and crystal gallery, followed by dedicated VIP chauffeur transfer to airport for onward flight.

Accommodation Details

5-Star Luxury Heritage Properties (The Imperial, ITC Mughal, Rambagh Palace, Taj Lake Palace). Daily gourmet breakfast and afternoon tea included.

Transportation Facilities

Private air-conditioned Toyota Innova Crysta / Mercedes-Benz E-Class with professional English-speaking chauffeur, toll passes, and mineral water.
